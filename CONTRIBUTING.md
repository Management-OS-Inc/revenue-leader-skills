# Contributing

Thank you for reading closely enough to want to improve these.

**Issues are welcome.** Report an error, a broken reference, or a passage that is unclear, and suggest improvements.

**Pull requests are not accepted.** Management OS maintains the skills so they stay consistent with each other and with the methodology behind them.

**Field cases are welcome through the field-case issue template.** Anonymize them completely: no names of people or companies, no figures that could identify either, nothing confidential. Submitting a case grants Management OS Inc. permission to adapt and publish it under this repository's license.
